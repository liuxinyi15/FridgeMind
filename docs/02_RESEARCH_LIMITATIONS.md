# Research Limitations

1. The supplied summary mixes people living alone (52/115) with those not living alone (63/115), so it cannot support target-population percentages without filtered raw data.
2. Four respondents did not meet the stated 18+ and consent condition; only supplied aggregates are available, so they cannot be removed from later statistics.
3. Percentages describe self-report, not observed behaviour, causality or feature effectiveness.
4. The questionnaire wording, sampling/recruitment method, geography and demographic distribution were not supplied.
5. Alex is a walkthrough construct, not a research persona.
6. Scoring weights, use-soon thresholds, intervention timing and confidence labels are unvalidated prototype heuristics.
7. Demo recognition is deterministic and does not measure real computer-vision accuracy.

Next research should filter consented target participants, conduct contextual inquiry around shopping/cooking, test comprehension and burden, and observe whether interventions change outcomes without creating anxiety or unsafe assumptions.
